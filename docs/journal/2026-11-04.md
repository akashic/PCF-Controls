**Discussion & decisions:**

## Session — 11:52

**Summary:** Wrote GridFormatter rules for the SMSF Matter table (`wbsuper_smsfmatter`) that colour the `wbsuper_stage` cell, a single line of text column, by its value. They started as a one-off config for the developer to deploy and ended up committed as two recipes in the control's `config/examples/`, one with solid pale backgrounds and black text, one with darker pills and white text.

**Discussion & decisions:**
- **White text on the requested pale colours was rejected in favour of two readable versions.** The first draft used the colours as asked, with white text. White on those fills is under 2:1, well below the 4.5:1 the control's contrast check warns at, so the developer asked for two alternatives: the same pale backgrounds with black text, and pills in darker shades of each colour with white text. The `#FFC7CE` value was given as rgb(255, 299, 206); 299 was a typo for 199.
- **Solid backgrounds need `"alpha": 1`.** A plain hex background shows at `tintAlpha` (18%), so the background version sets `{ "hex", "alpha": 1 }` to show the exact spreadsheet colours. The cost is that the grid's hover and selection colours are hidden on those cells; the pill version keeps them.
- **The pill shades keep each colour's hue but are darkened until white is at least 5.5:1**, since a pill picks black or white text itself and `color` is ignored. Yellow becomes dark gold at that darkness. When Audit's dark red came out nearly the same as Job Not In's `#B3192D`, Job Not In moved to maroon `#8F1424` to keep them apart.
- **Where to commit them** was put to the developer, because the repo has no home for project-specific configs: CLAUDE.md puts a table's rules in the project's solution, and the configuration guide says not to commit a local copy placed in `config/`. They chose recipes in `config/examples/`, which CI validates, keeping the real column name `wbsuper_stage`.
- **The default for any other value** is a rule with no `when`, an explicit `scope` and `"priority": 100`, so it runs after the value rules and only colours cells none of them matched. In the background version that includes empty cells; pills never draw on an empty cell.
